## Purpose and scope

This SOP defines authorization, advertising, shortlisting, assessment,
selection, pre-employment checks, offer approval, and onboarding handoff for
Contoso University employees. It applies to permanent, fixed-term, fractional,
and internally advertised roles. Agency workers, casual engagements, honorary
appointments, and contractor procurement follow separate procedures.

## Roles and responsibilities

- The **Hiring Manager** defines the need, manages the selection process, and
  documents decisions.
- The **Budget Holder** confirms funding for the full employment cost.
- The **People Partner** advises on organization design, grading, policy, and
  employment risk.
- The **Recruitment Team** publishes vacancies, manages candidates, and issues
  approved offers.
- The **Selection Panel** applies the published criteria consistently and
  declares conflicts of interest.
- The **Pre-employment Checking Team** verifies required identity, right to
  work, qualifications, references, and role-specific checks.

## Required inputs

Before advertising, the Hiring Manager must provide an approved vacancy
request, job description, person specification, grade, contract type, funding
code, work location, selection method, proposed panel, advertising approach,
and anticipated checks. Criteria must be necessary for the role, measurable,
and separated into essential and desirable requirements. Reasonable
adjustments and flexible working considerations must be addressed.

## Procedure

1. **Authorize the vacancy.** The Hiring Manager submits a request in the
   fictional Contoso Recruitment Portal. The Budget Holder confirms funding,
   and the People Partner confirms the employment model, grade, and whether
   redeployment or workforce-change obligations apply.
2. **Prepare inclusive materials.** The Hiring Manager and Recruitment Team
   review the job description, criteria, advert, assessment method, work
   location, and accessibility. Requirements that could indirectly exclude
   candidates must have a documented role-based justification.
3. **Advertise the role.** The Recruitment Team publishes the approved advert
   for the authorized period and records advertising channels. Changes to
   criteria after publication require People Partner approval and may require
   readvertising.
4. **Declare conflicts and shortlist.** Panel members declare actual,
   potential, or perceived conflicts. At least two trained panel members score
   each application against the published essential criteria and record
   evidence-based reasons.
5. **Arrange assessment.** The Recruitment Team invites candidates, requests
   adjustment information through a confidential route, and provides the same
   core assessment conditions and information to each candidate.
6. **Interview and assess.** The panel asks role-relevant questions, uses the
   agreed scoring framework, records evidence rather than impressions, and does
   not seek protected or irrelevant personal information.
7. **Select and approve.** The panel documents its scores, decision, reserve
   candidate if any, and reasons. The Hiring Manager obtains approval for any
   salary or terms outside the advertised and graded range before an offer.
8. **Issue a conditional offer.** Only the Recruitment Team issues the formal
   conditional offer. Verbal discussions must make clear that employment is
   subject to required checks and final authorization.
9. **Complete checks and hand off.** The Pre-employment Checking Team verifies
   checks proportionate to the role. When conditions are satisfied, the
   Recruitment Team confirms the start date and transfers the minimum necessary
   onboarding information to the employing department.

## Escalation and exceptions

A panel member with a material conflict must withdraw unless the People Partner
documents an effective mitigation. Suspected discrimination, manipulation of
criteria, inappropriate access to candidate information, or falsified evidence
is escalated to the Head of People Operations. Recruitment for a role involving
regulated activity, export controls, sensitive research, or substantial
financial authority cannot proceed without the designated checks. An urgent
staffing need does not permit an unauthorized appointment or a bypass of right
to work requirements.

## Records and controls

The Contoso Recruitment Portal is the authoritative record for authorization,
advertising, applications, adjustment handling, declarations, scores, reasons,
approvals, offers, and checks. Panel members must not keep local candidate
copies after records are secured in the portal. Access is role-based, and
records follow the fictional Recruitment Retention Schedule. People and Culture
reviews appointment outcomes, exceptions, time to hire, candidate experience,
and equality monitoring using appropriately restricted and aggregated data.
