## Purpose and scope

This SOP defines how Contoso University proposes, reviews, approves, publishes,
and periodically reviews new taught courses and modules. It applies to all
faculties and to undergraduate, postgraduate taught, online, and
apprenticeship provision. Minor editorial corrections that do not change
learning outcomes, credits, assessment, delivery mode, or resource demand may
use the separate catalogue correction process.

## Roles and responsibilities

- The **Proposal Lead** prepares the academic case and coordinates consultation.
- The **Head of Department** confirms strategic fit, staffing, and local funding.
- The **Faculty Quality Team** checks completeness and regulatory alignment.
- The **Faculty Education Committee** provides academic scrutiny and faculty
  approval.
- The **Academic Quality Office** performs the institutional compliance review.
- The **University Education Committee** grants final approval for new courses
  and material changes.
- The **Registry Curriculum Team** publishes approved records in the fictional
  Contoso Curriculum Catalogue.

## Required inputs

The proposal must include learning outcomes, credit value, level, syllabus,
teaching and assessment strategy, entry requirements, delivery locations,
student consultation, accessibility review, market evidence, resource and
library impact, placement requirements where applicable, and a teach-out plan
for replaced provision. The proposal must identify dependencies on partner
institutions, professional bodies, laboratories, specialist software, or
regulated activities.

## Procedure

1. **Initiate the proposal.** The Proposal Lead records the proposal in the
   fictional Curriculum Workflow Portal and selects either new course, new
   module, or material change. The portal assigns a proposal ID and target
   academic year.
2. **Complete consultation.** The Proposal Lead consults students, relevant
   departments, Registry, Library, Digital Learning, Timetabling, Disability
   Support, and external or professional stakeholders where applicable.
   Conflicts or unresolved dependencies are recorded rather than omitted.
3. **Secure departmental authorization.** The Head of Department confirms
   academic ownership, staffing capacity, budget, facilities, and alignment
   with the faculty plan. Rejected proposals are returned with reasons.
4. **Perform the quality check.** The Faculty Quality Team checks that learning
   outcomes align with level and assessment, credit totals are valid, inclusive
   learning requirements are addressed, and the proposal record is complete.
   Incomplete submissions return to the Proposal Lead.
5. **Conduct faculty scrutiny.** The Faculty Education Committee considers
   academic coherence, standards, duplication, demand, student experience, and
   operational feasibility. It may approve, approve subject to conditions,
   request revision, or reject.
6. **Complete institutional review.** For a new course or material change, the
   Academic Quality Office verifies regulations, external reference points,
   consumer information, data protection, accessibility, and any professional
   accreditation conditions.
7. **Obtain final approval.** The University Education Committee records its
   decision and any conditions. Approval is not effective until all conditions
   have named owners, evidence, and completion dates.
8. **Publish and implement.** The Registry Curriculum Team creates the
   authoritative catalogue record only after approval conditions are closed.
   Marketing, admissions, timetabling, and student systems must use that
   approved record.
9. **Review delivery.** The owning department monitors recruitment, attainment,
   feedback, accessibility, resource demand, and external examiner findings
   through annual monitoring and periodic review.

## Escalation and exceptions

A proposal must be escalated to the Academic Quality Director if it involves a
compressed approval timetable, delivery before final approval, unresolved
professional accreditation, a partner institution, a new country of delivery,
or a material risk to current students. No course or module may be advertised
as approved, opened for applications, or delivered while approval is pending.
An emergency timetable request does not waive academic or regulatory review.

## Records and controls

The Curriculum Workflow Portal is the authoritative record for submissions,
consultations, decisions, conditions, and approvals. Committee minutes must
reference the proposal ID. Access is role-based, and records are retained under
the fictional Academic Governance Retention Schedule. The Academic Quality
Office samples approved proposals annually and reports overdue conditions,
unauthorized catalogue changes, and recurring quality issues.
