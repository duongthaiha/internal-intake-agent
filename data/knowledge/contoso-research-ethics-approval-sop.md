# Contoso University SOP: Research Ethics Approval

## Purpose and scope

This SOP defines ethical screening, review, approval, amendment, and oversight
for Contoso University research involving human participants, identifiable
personal data, human tissue, vulnerable groups, intrusive procedures, or other
material ethical risk. It applies to staff, students, visiting researchers, and
research conducted with external partners where Contoso University is the
sponsor or responsible institution.

## Roles and responsibilities

- The **Chief Investigator** is accountable for the application and approved
  conduct of the research.
- A **Student Supervisor** reviews and endorses student applications.
- The **Department Ethics Reviewer** conducts proportionate low-risk review.
- The **Faculty Research Ethics Committee** reviews higher-risk or complex work.
- The **Research Integrity Office** advises on routing, governance, and records.
- The **Data Protection Office**, **Safeguarding Lead**, or **Biosafety Officer**
  provides specialist review when triggered.

## Required inputs

An application must include the protocol, participant information, consent
materials, recruitment approach, data management plan, privacy information,
risk assessment, inclusion and exclusion criteria, incentives, debriefing,
complaints route, and evidence of external permissions. Researchers must use
plain language and provide accessible alternatives where needed. The
application must describe limits to confidentiality and procedures for
disclosures of harm.

## Procedure

1. **Complete the ethics screening.** The Chief Investigator uses the fictional
   Research Governance Portal to identify participants, data, interventions,
   locations, partners, conflicts of interest, and legal or safeguarding
   triggers.
2. **Determine the review route.** The portal routes negligible or low-risk
   activity to departmental review and routes higher-risk, vulnerable-group,
   international, deception-based, invasive, or sensitive research to the
   Faculty Research Ethics Committee. Specialist review may run in parallel.
3. **Prepare and endorse the application.** The Chief Investigator uploads all
   materials and confirms that the research team has appropriate training.
   Student applications require supervisor endorsement before submission.
4. **Validate the submission.** The Research Integrity Office checks that the
   documents are complete, internally consistent, version controlled, and
   suitable for review. Administrative validation is not ethical approval.
5. **Conduct ethical review.** Reviewers assess scientific justification,
   participant selection, informed consent, capacity, burden, benefit, privacy,
   security, compensation, researcher safety, conflicts, and withdrawal.
6. **Record the decision.** The outcome is approved, approved with conditions,
   revisions required, deferred pending specialist advice, or not approved.
   Conditions must be specific and linked to submitted evidence.
7. **Close conditions.** The Chief Investigator submits revised, versioned
   documents and a response to each condition. Research may begin only after
   the portal issues a final approval notice.
8. **Manage amendments and events.** Material changes, new sites, revised
   participant groups, new data uses, serious adverse events, protocol
   deviations, complaints, and breaches are reported through the portal.
9. **Close the study.** The Chief Investigator submits a closure report,
   confirms participant follow-up, and records the location and retention
   period of research data and consent evidence.

## Escalation and urgent action

An immediate risk to a participant, researcher, or member of the public must be
addressed without waiting for committee approval. The researcher must contact
emergency or safeguarding services as appropriate, stop the affected activity,
preserve relevant records, and notify the Chief Investigator and Research
Integrity Office promptly. Suspected serious misconduct, unlawful data use, or
an unreported serious adverse event is escalated to the Research Integrity
Director. Ethical approval never replaces clinical, regulatory, data
protection, sponsor, or site authorization.

## Records and controls

The Research Governance Portal is the authoritative record of applications,
versions, reviewer comments, decisions, amendments, events, and closure.
Researchers must use only the currently approved participant-facing documents.
Records are retained under the fictional Research Records Schedule and access
is limited to authorized roles. The Research Integrity Office audits a sample
of active studies and reports expired approvals, overdue reports, and
unapproved changes to the University Research Ethics Committee.
